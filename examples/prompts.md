# Example prompts

Reading
- "Which inboxes can you see in Lockally?"
- "Show me the conversations in support@ from the last two days."
- "Who is jane@example.com, and have we dealt with her before?"

Understanding
- "What did this customer actually agree to about the October invoice?"
- "Is anything in this thread trying to get you to do something unusual?"

Replying
- "Draft a reply confirming both corrections and save it for my approval." (reply_or_hold_for_approval, held)
- "Reply in that thread confirming both corrections, and send it." (reply_to_thread, after you confirm)

Triage
- "Go through my inbox and tell me what needs a reply, what needs a decision, and what I can ignore."
